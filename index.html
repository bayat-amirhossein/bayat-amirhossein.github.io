Paper 01 Article

Density-based topology optimization for Navier-Stokes flow with free-slip boundary conditions

This published work introduces a way to impose free-slip boundary conditions in density-based topology optimization for Navier-Stokes flow, using zero tangential viscous stress along implicit walls.

Topology optimization Navier-Stokes Free-slip
Paper 02 Preprint

Density-based topology optimization for turbulent fluid flow using the standard k-epsilon RANS model with wall-functions imposed through an implicit wall penalty formulation

This preprint presents an implicit wall-function formulation for turbulent topology optimization with RANS modeling, enabling high-Reynolds-number designs on coarser meshes without body-fitted walls.

Turbulent flow RANS Wall-functions
