Hi, I am Amirhossein Bayat.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Southern Denmark, working on topology optimization for thermal-fluid systems. My research focuses on high-heat-flux cooling, turbulent flow modeling, conjugate heat transfer, and density-based optimization methods for fluid-flow design.

Selected research

Papers

Selected journal articles, preprints, and manuscripts connected to thermal-fluid topology optimization.

Paper 01 Article

Density-based topology optimization for Navier-Stokes flow with free-slip boundary conditions

Amirhossein Bayat, Hao Li, and Joe Alexandersen.

This published work introduces a way to impose free-slip boundary conditions in density-based topology optimization for Navier-Stokes flow, using zero tangential viscous stress along implicit walls.

Topology optimization Navier-Stokes Free-slip
Paper 02 Preprint

Density-based topology optimization for turbulent fluid flow using the standard k-epsilon RANS model with wall-functions imposed through an implicit wall penalty formulation

Amirhossein Bayat, Hao Li, and Joe Alexandersen.

This preprint presents an implicit wall-function formulation for turbulent topology optimization with RANS modeling, enabling high-Reynolds-number designs on coarser meshes without body-fitted walls.

Turbulent flow RANS Wall-functions
Paper 03 Under preparation

Topology optimization of high heat flux cooling using conjugate heat transfer

Amirhossein Bayat, Hao Li, and Joe Alexandersen.

A manuscript under preparation focused on thermal-fluid topology optimization for high-heat-flux cooling, using conjugate heat transfer to connect flow design with heat removal performance.

Conjugate heat transfer Cooling design Under preparation
